What FolioSpace is.

FolioSpace is a portfolio service for Indian professionals — chefs, students, consultants, developers, designers, and anyone with work to show. Right now we're in private beta: this website collects waitlist signups for the public launch on or around September 2, 2026.

What we promise.

  • We'll email you when we launch (and once or twice before that, no more).
  • We'll never sell or share your email.
  • We'll delete your data on request, and bulk-delete unconverted waitlist emails within 90 days of public launch.
  • We'll be honest about what's working and what's not.

What we don't promise.

  • A specific launch date — we aim for Sep 2, 2026, but if the product isn't ready, we slip.
  • Specific features or pricing that aren't listed on the site.
  • That the service will always be free or that prices won't change.

Acceptable use.

The FolioSpace brand, code, templates, and design system are ours. Don't copy the site wholesale or resell the templates as your own product. You're welcome to reference and learn from them.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to the fees you've paid us in the last 12 months (which, for the waitlist period, is zero).
