→ Questions

The honest answers.

Why does Year 2 cost more?

Year 1 is the launch offer — a 40% off thank-you for early adopters. From Year 2 the regular price is ₹{{ "{:,}".format(PRO_YEAR2_INR) }}. Refer 5 verified friends and we lock your renewal at the Year-1 price — every year, as long as you stay subscribed.

Do I need a card or netbanking?

No. Pro is paid by UPI AutoPay — PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, any UPI app. You approve a one-tap annual mandate once, then it auto-charges yearly. Card AutoPay also supported if you prefer.

What if my payment fails?

You get 7 grace days with full Pro access. We email you on day 1. If the charge still fails after day 7, your folio flips to Free — same template, with the FolioSpace badge.

Who handles the UPI mandate?

Cashfree Payments — a RBI-licensed payment aggregator. Your UPI mandate is created inside your UPI app, not by FolioSpace. We never see your UPI PIN or your account number.

What happens to my folio if I cancel?

Your handle (foliospace.in/yourname) is yours forever. If you cancel, your folio flips to Free: same content, badge visible, public toggle honored. Upgrade again any time.

Can I get a refund?

Cancel inside the first 7 days of a paid year and we refund the charge — email foliospace.in@gmail.com and we'll process it same-day.

Is my data safe?

Encrypted at rest (Neon Postgres + TLS in transit). You can delete your account any time — profile, folios, and visit history are wiped within 30 days. Full privacy policy →
