●FolioSpace · Issue 01 · Page 08/Questions

The stuff you're wondering.

Why ₹150 + ₹99/month?

Setup covers onboarding your first template + payment integration. The monthly fee keeps the lights on — UPI autopay, less than your chai. Cancel anytime in two taps.

Is this just another Linktree?

No. Linktree is a list of links. FolioSpace is a proper portfolio — case studies, a PDF that exports clean, contact forms that don't leak your email. Built for the Indian web, not retrofitted.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

We'll email you the moment launch slots open. First 100 get locked-in launch pricing, a founder badge, and a say in what we build next. No spam, no drip — just one ping when it's your turn.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes — Pro tier supports custom domains. Point your DNS, we handle the SSL. foliospace.in/yourname by default on Free.

Will my portfolio load on a 2G phone?

Yes. We obsess over Core Web Vitals and ship under 80KB on first load. Your page renders in under a second on a flaky network — that's the whole point.
