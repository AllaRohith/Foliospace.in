→ How a referral counts

Three boxes.

  1. 01
    They sign up via your link

    The /ref/{code} page drops a 30-day cookie. The signup form picks it up.

  2. 02
    They verify their email

    Their row flips from pending to verified. This happens the moment they click the OTP link.

  3. 03
    They publish their folio

    When they flip Make my folio public on, your count ticks up. That's the only moment a count can change.

→ Questions

The honest answers.

How long does the cookie last?

30 days. If they sign up within that window via your link, you get credit.

What if they don't make their folio public?

They don't count — the third box is unflipped. The default for every FolioSpace user is private, so this is opt-in on their end.

Do Free users count?

Yes. Anyone who clears the three boxes counts, regardless of plan.

What happens at 10?

If you're on a year-2 subscription, we swap you back to the year-1 plan immediately. If you're on year-1, the discount stays locked — every renewal, forever. If you're on Free, your first Pro year starts at ₹{{ "{:,}".format(PRO_YEAR1_INR) }}.

Can I lose the lock once I've earned it?

No. The lock is permanent. Cancelling and resubscribing still keeps the year-1 price.
