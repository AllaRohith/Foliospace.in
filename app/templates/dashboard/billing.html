→ Questions

The honest answers.

Why ₹149?

It covers payment processing, hosting, the OG image renderer, the QR generator, and the analytics storage — for one folio per user. We add features, not price hikes.

Do I need a card or netbanking?

No. Pro is paid by UPI AutoPay only — PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, any UPI app. You approve a one-tap mandate once, then it auto-charges monthly.

What if my payment fails?

You get 7 grace days with full Pro access. We email you on day 1 and day 5. If the charge still fails after day 7, your folio flips to Free — same template, with the FolioSpace badge.

Is the trial actually free?

Yes. No card, no mandate, no charge on day 14. We remind you by email at day 7, 4, and 2. If you don't activate, your folio goes to Free on day 15.

What happens to my folio if I cancel?

Your handle (foliospace.in/{{ user.slug or 'yourname' }}) is yours forever. If you cancel, your folio flips to Free: same content, badge visible, public toggle honored.

Can I get a refund?

Cancel inside the first 7 days of any paid month and we refund the charge — email foliospace.in@gmail.com and we'll process it same-day.
