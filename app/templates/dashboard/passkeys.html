→ Why a passkey
  • ›One tap. Face ID, Touch ID, or your device PIN — no typing.
  • ›Phishing-resistant. The credential is scoped to foliospace.in and can't be tricked into working elsewhere.
  • ›Syncs to your devices. iCloud Keychain and Google Password Manager carry it across — devices without a password manager (e.g., a hardware security key only) won't sync.
  • ›Email + password still works. You can sign in either way. Lost the device? Hit "Forgot?".
